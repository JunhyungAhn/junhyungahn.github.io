I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) School of Information Sciences, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Yonghan Jung.

Previously, I was an AI Engineer at NAVER, where I worked on off-policy evaluation for large-scale online ad auction systems and designed an LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation framework for automated quality assessment of AI-generated search responses, including bias mitigation strategies and an ensemble judging pipeline to replace human annotation at scale. I received my M.S. and B.S. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST, advised by Prof. Changho Suh.

Research interests

  • Causal inference: counterfactual reasoning, off-policy evaluation
  • Agentic AI: LLM-as-a-judge, evaluation bias mitigation
  • Recommender systems: data bias and scarcity, cold-start problems

selected publications

  1. ICDM
    On Predicting Post-Click Conversion Rate via Counterfactual Inference
    Junhyung Ahn and Sanghack Lee
    In IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM), 2025
    Best Paper Finalist Abs DOI arXiv Bib HTML

    Best Paper Award Finalist, IEEE ICDM 2025

    Conversion rate prediction suffers from selection bias: conversion labels are observed only for clicked impressions. We cast the missing labels as a counterfactual quantity and impute conversions for non-clicked samples via counterfactual inference, improving both offline estimation and online conversion rate.

    Matrix Completion with Hierarchical Graph Side Information
    Adel Elmahdy*, Junhyung Ahn*, Changho Suh, and 1 more author
    In Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2020
    Equal contribution.
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML PDF

    We consider a matrix completion problem that exploits social or item similarity graphs as side information, characterize the information-theoretic limit on the number of observed entries needed for exact recovery, and give a computationally efficient algorithm that achieves it.
